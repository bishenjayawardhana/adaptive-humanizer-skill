# Empirical AI vs. Human Stylometric Findings

Every quantitative rule in `SKILL.md` and `scripts/stylometry-audit.mjs` is anchored to **two complementary bodies of evidence**:
1. **Our Live OpenAlex 600-Paper Corpus Comparison** (`data/empirical-baselines.json`, generated by `scripts/corpus-research.mjs` on 2026-11-22): **300 verified pre-2022 human papers** (2015–2021, 61,665 words, 0% modern LLM contamination) vs. **300 post-2024 published papers** (2024–2026, 64,982 words) stratified evenly across 6 scholarly disciplines (50 per discipline), contrasted against a 6-domain **Raw-LLM baseline battery**.
2. **Peer-Reviewed Computational Linguistics & Detection Literature**: Kobak et al. (*Science Advances*, 2025) on LLM excess vocabulary across millions of PubMed abstracts; Liang et al. (Stanford) on lexical shifts and ESL false-positive bias; Reinhart et al. (2025); Wikipedia WikiProject AI Cleanup (*Signs of AI writing*); the **RAID** adversarial detection benchmark; and zero-shot/neural detector research (**Binoculars**, **Fast-DetectGPT**, **GPTZero**, **Pangram**, **Originality.ai**, **Copyleaks**, **ZeroGPT**).

## 3. Why Modern Detectors Catch Naive "Humanizers"

Synthesizing the RAID benchmark, Binoculars, Fast-DetectGPT, and commercial benchmark audits (ZeroGPT, GPTZero, Pangram, Originality.ai, Copyleaks):

1. **Statistical Curvature vs. Surface Synonyms**: Zero-shot detectors like *Binoculars* and *Fast-DetectGPT* measure whether a passage sits at a local token-probability peak of reference LLMs. Swapping isolated adjectives (*crucial* → *essential*) leaves the underlying syntactic tree and conditional probability curve almost untouched.
2. **Adversarial Perturbation Classifiers**: Commercial neural detectors (*Pangram*, *Originality.ai*, updated *GPTZero*) explicitly train on paraphrased AI (QuillBot, basic humanizers) and synthetic typo injections. Superficial synonym cycling or artificial misspellings now raise red flags rather than lowering them.
3. **The Clustering Effect**: No single word proves AI generation. Detectors fire strongly when **4+ weak signals converge simultaneously**—medium-uniform sentence length + explicit additive openers + trailing participle commentary + symmetrical paragraphs + elevated `MATTR` + RLHF hedging.
4. **What Actually Shifts Text Into the True Human Manifold**:
   - **Macro-level rhetorical restructuring** (opening with concrete evidence/tension instead of a panoramic hook, dropping bow-tied summary sentences, uneven paragraph pacing).
   - **Semantic-first clause re-authoring** (building fresh syntactic trees with genuine short/long burstiness and plain-spoken domain verbs).
   - **Authentic authorial agency** (calibrated first-person where discipline-appropriate, specific named citations instead of vague attributions, explicit methodological limits).
   - **Natural noun repetition** (bringing `MATTR-50` and average word length back into the genuine `0.74–0.85` / `5.3–6.2 char` human zone).
